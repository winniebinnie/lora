Run04 tested outdoor line-of-sight at approximately 100 cm using the same radio configuration as previous runs. The result showed strong agreement between device-side and network-side RSSI-derived bitstrings. BDR was 1.21%, much lower than Run02 and Run03. Usable bits reached 248 from 987 packet pairs, with entropy remaining high. This suggests that outdoor LOS or cleaner propagation conditions are favorable for RSSI-assisted bootstrapping.
