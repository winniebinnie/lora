Run03 tested indoor dynamic movement while keeping the same nominal distance and radio configuration as Run01 and Run02. Compared with indoor static runs, dynamic movement reduced performance significantly. The analyzer selected window=1 and alpha=1.0, produced only 72 usable bits, and had a high discard rate of 92.7%. BDR increased to 19.44%, indicating that movement introduced channel variation but reduced agreement between the two legitimate RSSI sequences. This suggests that uncontrolled indoor movement may not improve the proposed method unless probe timing, calibration, or quantization strategy is improved.
